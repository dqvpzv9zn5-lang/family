How it all connects

Every notable person in the tree is connected to you through one of these four chains. All routes run through your mother June's side of the family.

All of the royal and noble connections discovered so far run through June Fyfe (Steve's mum), back through the Fyfe, Paterson, Blair, and Montgomery lines in Clackmannanshire and Ayrshire, Scotland. The chains then branch in different directions — some into Scottish royalty, some into English royalty, some into the great noble families of medieval Yorkshire and Northumberland.

Living Connections

Because the Young family shares Robert the Bruce as a documented ancestor, there are traceable — if very distant — connections to several well-known people alive today. These are all through the same Bruce/Stewart line.

The key to understanding these connections: Robert the Bruce is your 22nd great-grandfather. He is King Charles III's 20th great-grandfather. That two-generation difference means you and Charles share a common ancestor but are in different branches — making you very distant cousins. The exact cousin relationship depends on the generations, but think of it as "distant cousins separated by 700 years" — real, documented, but extremely remote.
